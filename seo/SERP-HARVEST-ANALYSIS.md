## THE FORMULA (why they're on page 1 — 100% of results)

1. **Tool page, not article.** Zero pure blog posts rank for generator/extractor/checker keywords. 100% transactional tool intent.
2. **Exact keyword in URL + H1 + title.** No exceptions.
3. **Tool + education, sized by authority.** Big domains: 247–1,000 words is enough. Small/new domains: 900–3,600 words. Small-site floor ≈ 900–1,000 words (timeskip, TubeRanker).
4. **Tools HUB with sibling interlinks.** Every winner links 3+ sibling tools. No lone-page winners.
5. **Differentiator wedge in title.** [FREE] / Shorts / by-link / copy-paste / no-login.
6. **FAQ block.** Most winners.
7. **(How-to SERPs only)** tutorial videos + Chrome Web Store listings also rank — extra surfaces.
8. **AI Mode/answer engines** cite the consensus entities (Rapidtags, vidIQ) — consensus earned via years of mentions, not on-page SEO.
